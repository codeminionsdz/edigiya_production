Sales Service
─────────────
  processOrderSale()
    • Create sales order
    • Deduct stock using FIFO (atomic via PostgreSQL)
    • Record cash transaction
    • Returns: success status & details

  deductStockFIFOAtomic()
    • Call PostgreSQL deduct_stock_fifo() function
    • Atomically deduct stock from oldest batches
    • Record stock movements
    • Returns: {success, total_deducted, batches_used}

  getCurrentCashBalance()
    • Get current cash balance
    • Uses: cash_ledger table

Purchase Service
────────────────
  createPurchase()
    • Create purchase order
    • Auto-generate PO number
    • Calculate totals

  receiveStock()
    • Receive stock from supplier
    • Create stock batches (FIFO tracking)
    • Record stock movements

Stock Service
─────────────
  getStockLevel()
    • Get current stock by product
    • Uses: stock_levels materialized view

  getProductBatches()
    • Get FIFO ordered batches
    • Sorted by received_date

  recordStockAdjustment()
    • Record adjustments (damage, loss, etc.)

Cash Service
────────────
  getCashBalance()
    • Get current balance

  recordTransaction()
    • Record cash in/out

  validateLedgerIntegrity()
    • Verify cash balance is correct
